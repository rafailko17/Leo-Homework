Name of the Game: "Little Dino Tales"

Game Description: On a beautiful summer day, Ari, a young dinosaur, plays carefree with his toys outside of his family's cave. His father has passed away, but he still has his mother and two sisters. Very intuitive as he is, he senses something hazardous approaching. Puts his toys away and looks up to the sky. It's bright, it's burning and rapidly approaching his family's territory. Runs around shouting about the imminent threat, but no one believes him. So he decides to save the ones he truly cares about the most. But in order to achieve his goal, he needs to try different methods which are not easy to come up with.

Player: The player will have objectives and will use various tools to build an escape means of transport, will use weapons to fight enemies on his way to safety, and will have to solve puzzles in order to move on.

Alternative Escape Plans: Escape with a raft, fly a plane away from the place the comet will hit, shoot the comet with a weapon you need to construct, befriend the enemies on your way to help you succeed.
